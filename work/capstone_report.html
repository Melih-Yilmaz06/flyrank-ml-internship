Content Opportunity & Decay Scoring: A Machine Learning Approach to Proactive SEO

Author: Melih Yılmaz
Lane: Refresh / Content Opportunity Scoring
Date: August 2026
Target Split: 80/20 Stratified Test (N=1,769)
TL;DR / Executive Summary
  • The Question: What pages should we review first before traffic loss impacts business revenue?
  • The Data: Client-anonymized daily performance logs (8,844 labeled pages, 14-day observation vs. 14-day outcome windows).
  • The Result: Model F1: 0.5124 (Recall 51.69%) vs Baseline F1: 0.2828 (Recall 21.35%) — an 81.2% lift in detection efficiency.
  • The Decision Support: A triaged Action Playbook sorting decaying pages into Title/Meta Rewrite (94.9%), Content Quality Refresh (4.9%), or Watch / Competitor Analysis (0.2%).
  • The Biggest Limitation: Predictive only, no causal claims. A predictive feature is not a search ranking factor.

1. Problem Framing

Organic search traffic is highly volatile. By the time an editorial team observes a macro-level traffic drop on a high-value URL, revenue and keyword rankings have already slipped. This project delivers a predictive decision-support system to prioritize proactive content refreshes.

  • Unit of Analysis: Individual web pages, tracked pseudonymously via content_hash_id.
  • Decision Supported: Allocating limited editorial hours to URLs showing early, non-obvious decay signals before severe traffic collapse.
  • Output: A ranked queue assigning each predicted decaying page an operational Reason Code with concrete editorial next steps.
  • Cost of a Wrong Call: False positives waste editorial bandwidth on healthy pages; false negatives result in unmitigated traffic loss. Our ML triage directly reduces missed decaying content relative to heuristic alerts.

2. Data Safety & Leakage Prevention

Data was ingested from the warehouse table fact_content_daily_performance covering 8,844 content items with minimum traffic thresholds (previous_clicks >= 10). Strict data hygiene and leakage prevention rules were enforced:

  • Strict Temporal Separation: All predictive features are computed exclusively from the 14-day observation window (previous_window). The target label is derived strictly from the subsequent 14-day outcome window (current_window).
  • Exclusion of Leaky Fields: Outcome metrics such as curr_clicks, curr_impressions, and click_change_pct were strictly excluded from feature matrices.
  • Identifier Stripping: Pseudonymous keys (client_hash_id, content_hash_id) were used solely for window aggregations and dropped prior to model training.
  • Privacy: No raw URLs, search queries, client names, or PII are retained or exposed.

3. Baseline Heuristic

In production SEO workflows, teams rely on static rule-based alerts. We benchmarked against a standard industry heuristic rule:

Predict Decay (1) IF prev_ctr < 0.01 AND prev_avg_position > 10.0

While transparent, this rule fails to capture subtle decay patterns, catching only 21.35% of truly decaying URLs on our test split.

4. Model Architecture & Training

We trained an XGBoost Classifier (eval_metric='logloss', random_state=42) to capture non-linear interactions across traffic volume, CTR slippage, and engagement metrics.

  • Target Label (is_decaying): Binary flag indicating a ≥ 20% decline in clicks between the observation window and the outcome window (click_change_pct <= -0.20). Base rate in test set: 48.45%.
  • Engineered Features (Observation Window Only): prev_clicks, prev_impressions, prev_ctr, prev_avg_position, prev_engagement_rate, and prev_organic_ratio.
  • Validation Protocol: Stratified 80/20 train/test split (Train: 7,075 rows, Test: 1,769 rows). Both baseline and model are evaluated on the exact same test records.

Evaluation Takeaways

  • Recall Improvement: XGBoost captured 51.69% of decaying URLs compared to only 21.35% from the heuristic rule—over 2.4× more decaying pages identified before severe traffic collapse.
  • Accuracy Context: Absolute classification accuracy sits near 52.35%. External search engine algorithm shifts, seasonal macro-trends, and competitor updates introduce substantial irreducible noise. However, for a prioritization triage tool, Recall and F1 are the critical metrics.

6. Model Interpretation & Feature Importance

Gini feature importance analysis demonstrates a well-distributed model relying on multivariate signals rather than a single dominant shortcut:

  • prev_organic_ratio (18.0%): Dependency on organic search versus multi-channel traffic is the strongest indicator of exposure to algorithm fluctuations.
  • prev_clicks (16.9%) & prev_impressions (16.3%): Baseline volume scale provides necessary context for demand stability.
  • prev_avg_position (17.0%): Ranking distance from the top 3 positions where CTR drops non-linearly.
  • prev_engagement_rate (16.6%) & prev_ctr (15.3%): User satisfaction and snippet relevance provide early qualitative decay indicators.

7. Decision Support: Ranked Action Playbook

Model predictions are transformed into an operational queue of 857 flagged pages, sorted descending by prev_clicks so editorial teams protect high-traffic assets first. Each page is mapped to a prescriptive action:

1. Title/Meta Rewrite 94.9% of flagged pages (795 URLs)

Trigger: prev_ctr < 0.02 despite healthy impressions.

Action: Refresh title tags, refine meta descriptions, align snippet copy with current search intent, and implement schema markup.

2. Content Quality Refresh 4.9% of flagged pages (32 URLs)

Trigger: prev_engagement_rate < 0.10.

Action: Update outdated statistics, add structural subheadings, improve page load performance, and embed multimedia.

3. Watch & Competitor Analysis 0.2% of flagged pages (5 URLs)

Trigger: Traffic drops despite strong baseline CTR and engagement.

Action: Audit SERP changes for new SERP features, AI overviews, or aggressive competitor updates; maintain monitoring.

8. Ranked Recommendations

  1. Immediate Triage: Deploy the top 50 ranked URLs by prev_clicks into the weekly editorial sprint for title/meta optimization.
  2. Workflow Integration: Replace static heuristic alerts with the model-generated weekly priority queue to eliminate missed decay.
  3. Continuous Calibration: Retrain the model on 30-day sliding windows to adapt to ongoing search engine updates.

9. Limitations

Predictive vs. Causal Distinction: This model is predictive, not causal. A predictive feature is not a search ranking factor. We observed and measured decay, but we make no causal claim that fixing a page causes traffic recovery. That requires a controlled A/B test.

  • Irreducible Search Noise: External factors such as broad Google core updates, competitor content launches, and seasonal query volume shifts cannot be fully observed in historical performance logs alone.
  • Short Observation Windows: A 14-day observation window may introduce sensitivity to transient day-of-week seasonality or temporary crawl issues.
  • Imputed GA4 Engagement: Pages without active GA4 integrations were zero-imputed, slightly dampening engagement signal resolution for uninstrumented clients.
